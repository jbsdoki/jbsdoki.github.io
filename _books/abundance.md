This book is a philosophical argument about the future of the US. It describes America as stagnant and incapable of building, and offers the solution as cutting back on the regulations and proceedings that prevents creation and construction. They juxtapose failures of innovation and government, like the California High Speed Rail project, along with successes, like Operation Warp Speed, the COVID vaccine development program. The book ends with a hopeful thesis, that if we allow the government to get out of its own way and slash the red tape, we can build a future that resembles a science fiction utopia.
